GEOMETRY BASIS
The plans are orthographic projections of the original exported triangle
meshes, not drawings from guessed object dimensions. Projected triangles are
unioned to recover each object's footprint. Axes have equal physical scale.
The render uses those same meshes and the aperture display meshes extracted
from the prepared Blender projects. The source models retain their existing
approximation of the laboratory; accurate reproduction of the model does not
turn an estimated laboratory dimension into a measured one.

The single-RIS configuration is taken from the corrected 13 October run:
RIS_Radpour_Benchmark_2026-10-13/runs/lab_virtual_probe/reflective_P1
and its P2 counterpart, with the corrected 1,380-point receiver grid.
The four-RIS configuration is taken from:
RIS_4RIS_NoPlant_Baseline_2026-10-13/runs/lab_virtual_probe/reflective_figure_xy.
The stand reflection comes from the later object-attribution audit and its
native cached TX, reflection and receiver coordinates.

The supplied ElementPositions RIS2cAll_2 drawing was inspected for the four-tile
arrangement and coordinate orientation. IAT_Lab_Blueprint was inspected for
room dimensions. Laboratory photos were used to check the object arrangement.
Their approximate appearance was not substituted for the saved mesh geometry.

COORDINATES AND STRAIGHT PATHS
Horizontal plot axis is paper y; vertical axis is paper x, increasing downward.
For the adopted model frame:
  paper y = RIS centre world X - world X
  paper x = world Y - RIS centre world Y
Front views use paper y and height relative to the RIS centre.
Cell positions, target positions and receiver arrays are read directly from
the saved configurations. The complete 127 / 508 cell sets are plotted.
The receiver plans contain all 1,380 / 2,300 sample points.

Each propagation leg is a straight line between its saved 3D endpoints.
The stand route consists of two straight segments joined at the actual cached
reflection point. No curved paths or invented reflection vertices are used.
Segment/triangle tests check for interior occlusions. The direct TX-target
diagnostic is drawn only up to its FIRST actual blocker intersection, ending
with an X. A crossing of a footprint in the top projection need not imply a
3D obstruction: object and ray heights are included in the visibility check.
The single-RIS 1.86 m and 1.40 m annotations are three-dimensional distances,
not lengths measured in the horizontal projection.

IMPORTANT: SINGLE-RIS DISPLAY REGISTRATION
Inspection found that the archived single-RIS support and aperture display
have identity world transforms and vertices local to the origin, while the
saved RIS_REFERENCE anchor and numerical cells are at approximately
(1.65, 0.15, 1.39) m. The stand consequently extends below the floor in that
archived file. The four-RIS geometry does not have this discrepancy.

For the FIGURE COPY ONLY, the single-RIS support and aperture display are
translated by the saved RIS anchor. This restores the placement explicitly
defined by the model's anchor; no RF map, fitted position or photo-derived
offset was used. Object dimensions and orientation are preserved. The
translation is disclosed on figure 2 and in display_registration.json.
The original Blender file, exported simulation scene, RIS states, materials
and reported results have NOT been changed. Therefore the illustration must
not be described as evidence that the archived single-RIS room-export bug has
already been repaired or its propagation effect assessed.

RENDERING SIMPLIFICATIONS
The 3D figure is an orthographic cutaway. Side/front walls, ceiling and overhead
services are omitted from the illustration for visibility; their source mesh
data remain in the archived geometry. The exact omissions are stored in each
figure scene. Grayscale display colours are cosmetic, not modified RF
materials. Grid guides in the 3D inset are sparsified for visibility; full
receiver sample coordinates are plotted in the top views. The TX phase centre
and target are small numerical markers, not added antenna hardware. Standalone
top views should be used with the corresponding complete figure caption.

SUGGESTED CAPTION 1
External cell-based RIS workflow using Sionna RT 2.1. (a) Equal-scale top
projection of the saved TX, representative surface cell, design target and
receiver grid. (b) All 127 cell centres and the PCB outline from the source
model. (c) Incoming and outgoing channels are traced before selecting the
constrained cell responses at the target. The responses remain fixed during
the scan. Cell ports are driven by the incident channel; they are not
independent transmitters. The optional background and controlled RIS channels
are combined coherently. Physical source power enters once through
P_RX = P_TX |h_eff|^2; h_eff is a channel coefficient, not an electric field.

SUGGESTED CAPTION 2
Single-RIS benchmark geometry at 23.8 GHz. (a) Equal-scale model plan showing
the TX, direct-link blocker, scanner, exact receiver grid and alternative P1
and P2 target configurations. All displayed propagation legs are straight
projections of the saved 3D endpoints. The annotated 1.86 m incident and 1.40 m
target distances are three-dimensional lengths. (b) Orthographic cutaway of
the source mesh geometry. (c) Exact 127-cell layout. The scan uses the adopted
30 by 46 reference-grid convention at 2 cm spacing. The principal benchmark
tests the LoS RIS branch; surrounding geometry explains laboratory context
and visibility, without asserting calibrated multipath validation. The
single-RIS display support/aperture is registered to its saved RIS anchor in
the figure copy only, as disclosed above.

SUGGESTED CAPTION 3
Current four-RIS, no-plant Case 3 baseline. (a) Equal-scale plan of the saved
model with all 2,300 receivers. Solid paths show a selected TX-cell-target
route; the gray dashed route is the cached TX-metal-support-RX reflection
associated with the false simulated peak. The reflection point is an actual
path vertex, not a freely placed graphical control point. The direct-link
segment ends at its first blocker intersection. (b) Orthographic model
cutaway. (c) All 508 cells in the supplied four-tile arrangement. The adopted
receiver height is 0.96 m and the RIS centre height is 1.44 m. Actual scanner
phase-centre registration, experimental RIS states and power calibration
remain unconfirmed. These illustrations preserve the adopted geometry and
do not establish successful measurement validation.

REPRODUCTION
prepare_geometry.py reads original archived configurations and PLY exports.
inspect_blend.py reads prepared source .blend files and extracts aperture
display meshes; it records the single-RIS transform discrepancy.
render_model.py creates figure-only Blender scenes and orthographic renders.
draw_schematics.py creates the coordinate plans, cell views, PNG/SVG and PDF.
verify_and_package.py checks geometry, grayscale output, source preservation
and archive integrity. NumPy/Matplotlib and the existing addon-vendored Shapely
are used; Blender 5.2 renders the cutaways. No new RF simulations are performed.
